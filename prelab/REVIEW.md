# Pre-lab review outcome

The intent is to identify which actual C220 versus C270 conclusions follow from declared inputs and what still requires a lab observation. Physical qualification, continuous uncertainty guarantees and a general finite-pad wetting solver are outside the established result.

## Independent reviews

[Review A](review-a.md) and [review B](review-b.md) independently checked conversion, native data, scenario accounting, coupled mechanics and claims. [Finite review](finite-review.md) independently reconstructed curved patches and reactions from raw energies. Reviewers used the configured inherited model. These are independent agents, not a claim of model diversity or external scientific peer review.

Review A found that replay could accept matching values with wrong input or engine metadata. Finite review then found that an accepted result could omit its native engine object. Both findings were accepted. The shared validator now requires declared inputs, input hash, producer engine and the native engine identity for a numerically checked result. Replay rechecks source and engine after all workers complete.

The end-to-end regression substitutes worker receipts that preserve gaps and forces while corrupting load, input hash, producer engine, native engine or the native-engine field's presence. Every forgery is rejected. The campaign verifier also rejects a rehashed accepted receipt with a missing engine field. The complete 61-request campaign and thirteen-request replay were rerun after the repair. All sixty accepted numerical results match the earlier campaign exactly. The rejected control remains rejected.

No unit-conversion or mechanical-balance issue remained in the reviewed native campaign or rigid-cap examples. No actionable finite-model science issue was found within its restricted assumptions. Comment review found no scoped source comments or correctness suppressions requiring removal.

## Limits that remain open

The finite-pad prototype still fails native moving-height optimization for C270. The explicit penetrating control remains intrusive and not converged. It receives no physical eligibility. An inactive sampled surface is not proof of continuous solid exclusion, uniqueness, global minimum or correct wetting energy at new contact.

The 10,000 micronewton request is a retained native error rejected by the gate. It does not prove that every physical equilibrium at that load is impossible. The field name in the frozen protocol is not a mathematical no-equilibrium certificate.

All input variation is declared geometry or unqualified stress. No measured process distribution, coverage probability or physical discrepancy bound was inferred. The three micrometer measurement target and five micrometer future model-error target are not demonstrated capability.

Hot wetting, actual liquid volume, material, whole-cap geometry, load sharing and hot-to-cold transformation still require measurement. The raw data and fixed-gap controls support conditional numerical statements only. A same-process pilot remains the next source of physical evidence.
