# Pre-lab design synthesis

The base is the coupon-specific paired scenario runner from `uncertainty.md`. Its output keeps ranking, 185 micrometer numerical clearance, whole-cap physical clearance and liquid-model qualification separate. Its inputs contain no confirmatory outcomes.

The finite-pad experiment from `boundary.md` is a bounded graft. A separate child process can alter the native generator locally to enforce explicit copper and substrate exclusion. It carries prescribed wetting depths, conserves liquid volume by an identified envelope, disables gravity, retains unchanged native numerical checks, and rejects active-contact physical interpretations. It does not change the application model or prospective lab freeze.

The organizing record is a paired physical scenario. It expands into complete arm requests. Each native result retains request, boundary, source and engine identities. The native worker exits after one result. The campaign parent owns receipt accounting and interpretation. There is no new production solver setting or service.

The research adapter must expose `FinitePadRequest`, `envelope_spec`, `solve_finite_pad`, and `fixed_energy_check`. A request contains liquid `LoadSpec`, lower and upper metal thickness, and lower and upper prescribed wetting depth. The adapter cannot accept gravity. It cannot silently assign the envelope's density to real copper. Results with active new copper contact are geometrical experiments only.

The runner compares the two established boundary mappings at sixteen declared paired scenarios. It confirms the opposite-specification endpoints and wider opposite-volume stress natively. A small shared-depth scan identifies clearance crossings. Arm-specific wetting states are reported as explicit hypotheses, never as a measured process distribution. The six archived nominal values are an independent baseline gate.

The full movable-contact-line and hysteresis model is rejected for this phase because the current evidence identifies neither its parameters nor its observed contact state. A new optimizer, Rust port, and general uncertainty framework are also rejected. These would add complexity without settling the missing boundary observation.

Verification includes conclusion tampering, missing receipts, changed source identity, boundary-volume controls, nominal native replay, decision-critical native replay, numerical failures, and independently relaxed energy derivatives. No sampled extrema become certified interval bounds. A reported negative result satisfies the decision question when it names the input or physical observation that prevents qualification.
