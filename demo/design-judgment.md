Use B as the base. It gives an engineer the comparison before the experiment form. Its read-only page also keeps recorded evidence out of the existing workbench's mutable result lifecycle.

This is a source-based design review. It did not run native computation or inspect an implemented page. Visual scores assess the specified interaction, not measured usability.

Experience First changes the entry point to the focused comparison. Laziness Protocol keeps the existing case editor, solver, archive writer, and replay command. A's strongest argument is reduced editing friction. That advantage does not justify extending `app.js` into a second comparison mode for this one demonstration.

## Grafts

1. Keep A's explicit conditional screen beside the first result. State that the objective is lateral restoration without upward force at the prescribed gap. Show whether each case meets that declared screen. A broad question about whether more solder "helps" is insufficient without that objective.
2. Keep A's acceptance check for the editing transition. Download the selected reference and candidate, open the actual file in the existing workbench, and verify both volumes and the shared conditions. Editing must invalidate any imported result through the existing mechanism. Do not add another editor or a parallel invalidation state to B.

## Reconcile the new extreme probes

Use three paired offsets of 40, 120, and 200 µm plus the separate compressed failure in the final seven-case archive. Preserve the earlier 80 µm receipt in the historical probe archive. The final page must identify its curated samples rather than imply the historical experiment had only seven cases.

The compressed case needs its own scenario identity and gap value. Offset alone cannot identify it because its 40 µm offset also exists at the ordinary 150 µm gap. Keep this failure outside the ordinary paired-offset selection or extend the reader's key to include the prescribed gap. Never substitute its failed result into the ordinary 40 µm pair.

At 200 µm, the larger-volume result passed numerical refinement but extends beyond the wetted pad planes according to `probe-extremes.log`. Present its exact geometry and warning. Withhold the favorable pair outcome. Its changed axial-force sign must not become a recommendation because substrate clearance is unmodeled. The compressed case must retain its captured engine failure and receive no solved mesh or favorable force interpretation.

The 120 µm pair is the best opening sample. Its retained results show different axial-force directions without the new 200 µm scope warning. Keep 200 µm and the compressed failure readily accessible so the extreme demonstration also shows where interpretation ends.

## Implementation constraints

Prefer a small exact-mesh helper only if extraction leaves the old workbench's state ownership intact. If extraction requires broader restructuring, accept a bounded demo-local renderer and record that choice. A renderer framework is unnecessary.

The same archive must supply the visible forces, mesh, source footprint, and export. Verify semantic pairing after ordinary archive integrity. The first viewport must show the held gap, recorded status, isolated-joint scope, and physical qualification limit. A footprint view supplies context. Repeated solder meshes would imply an unsupported array solve.

Ship only after the actual downloaded archive passes trusted replay, all retained cases match the page, tampering is rejected, the separate failure remains visible, and the existing workbench still opens the exported editable case.
