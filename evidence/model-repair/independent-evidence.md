# Independent physical evidence for a PCB decision

The bounded search found a second physical geometry series that fits SolderShape's circular-pad scope. It did not find a new independently calibrated molten-solder force series with all required inputs. The next decisive test is the assembled stand-off change caused by changing the substrate pad diameter.

## Selected source and frozen extraction

[Van Veen, Vandevelde and Beyne, 2004](https://imapsjmep.org/article/39820.pdf), printed pages 60 and 61, supplies Table 3 and two cross-section comparisons. The input is 0.01745 mm³ eutectic solder, a 280 µm chip pad, 34 µm substrate copper, NSMD substrate pads, and approximately 4 µN chip load per ball. The authors add the substrate copper-pad volume, calculate total height, then subtract 34 µm because measured stand-off spans the bond pads. Only one copper cylinder is specified. Figure 9 reports approximately 60 µm misalignment for the 170 µm substrate-pad case. Flux, temperature, same-process surface tension, volume uncertainty, height uncertainty, and most offsets are missing. No statistical physical pass follows from this source alone.

## Decisive comparison

Use the published pad approximation first. For substrate diameter `q` in millimeters, set the lower diameter to `q`, upper diameter to `0.280`, and effective volume to `0.01745 + pi*(q/2)^2*0.034` mm³. Find a gap whose upward upper-pad reaction is 4 µN. Subtract 0.034 mm from that gap before comparing to the measured rim stand-off.

Run all four diameters at zero offset. Preserve the 170 µm case at the independently reported approximate 60 µm offset as a separate comparison. Zero offset in the other rows is an assumption, because the source does not report their offsets. A separate literal-rim sensitivity uses actual solder volume 0.01745 mm³ and rim gap directly. Do not combine the two mapping corrections.

Use surface tensions 0.35, 0.40, 0.48, and 0.55 N/m without fitting. These are stress scenarios, not a measured process interval. Also solve zero applied load with gravity disabled. That geometry is independent of surface tension. A result requiring a specific fitted surface tension remains calibration-dependent.

Report every micrometer residual and every numerical or geometric failure. Preserve the full measured ordering. The measured reduction from 270 to 320 µm pads is 15 µm. An illustrative 185 µm minimum clearance lies between the measured 192 and 177 µm heights. A model can assist that choice only if the predicted ordering survives material and geometry assumptions. A qualified production recommendation also needs process uncertainty smaller than the remaining clearance margin. The publication does not supply that uncertainty.

## The strongest practical alternative

For aligned circular pads and small chip load, compare against the paper's truncated-sphere formula. It solves a volume relation with a small load correction. It needs fewer numerical concepts and already predicts the source series closely. A full mesh solver must not claim an advantage merely for reproducing this easy regime.

For nonzero offsets or substantial external load, direct native Surface Evolver with the correct contact model is the numerical alternative. The [NIST load-driven implementation](https://www.ctcms.nist.gov/solder/underfill/index.html) already solves stand-off and offset together. SolderShape can add value through footprint input, explicit geometry definitions, admissibility checks, uncertainty comparisons, and replayable exports. It must agree with that upstream model before attributing a mismatch to physics.

## Other primary sources checked

- [Jesudoss et al., 2008](https://cora.ucc.ie/server/api/core/bitstreams/26a09f61-c21b-4e2d-8827-1e2bead11d49/content) includes an observed rectangular-pad cross-section and simulated volume changes. Its force and concave-versus-convex volume illustrations are calculations. The physical comparison is qualitative and uses a different pad geometry. It is not a new measured circular-joint force dataset.
- [Krammer, 2014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.microrel.2013.10.010) compares deliberately misplaced passive components before and after soldering. Publisher experiment snippets support the physical alignment comparison. The reported 450 and 150 µN restoring forces are model outputs. Sidewall metallization changes the boundary geometry. These numbers cannot validate SolderShape's force scale.
- [Lo et al., 2008](https://doi.org/10.1109/ECTC.2008.4550239) reports physical stand-off comparisons while varying pad geometry and package weight. Its [author-institution record](https://researchportal.hkust.edu.hk/en/publications/determination-of-solder-bump-stand-off-height-in-a-flip-chip-sub-/) supplies no specimen table. No measurement was extracted from the abstract.

The [2017 capillary self-alignment review](https://acris.aalto.fi/ws/portalfiles/portal/11230847/Surface_tension_driven_self_alignment_Sof_matter.pdf) helped trace primary references. It supplies no independent scoring observations here. The search stopped after these six strong source targets, including NIST and the selected 2004 paper.

## Stop condition and a real-process check

If numerical agreement, pad mapping, and the independently reported offset do not resolve disagreement, retain the failed comparison. Do not add free wetting parameters or tune each specimen's volume to obtain agreement. If unknown process variation can reverse the pad choice, the correct product output is an unresolved decision.

A same-process coupon is the smallest route to qualified design advice. Manufacture the two candidate pad sizes using measured solder volumes and the intended alloy, flux, and reflow profile. Measure pad dimensions, deposited volume, package load and post-reflow stand-off on repeated assemblies. Cross-section representative joints to check wetting and voids. Use one designated control geometry to calibrate material assumptions if needed, then predict the other geometry without fitting it. Freeze the clearance requirement and measurement uncertainty before inspecting predictions. The decision passes only if the independent prediction and measured clearance margin remain on the same side of the requirement after uncertainty. Otherwise improve the identified input or reject the equilibrium decision model for that process.
