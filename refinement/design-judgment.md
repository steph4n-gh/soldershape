# Choose the focused decision study

Candidate B is the recommended base. It represents two actual footprint revisions and one engineering requirement in a single study. Candidate A is a useful proposed-diameter calculator, but its candidate diameter does not derive from a candidate footprint. That distinction matters for a colleague checking which board revision produced the result.

This judgment reads both completed candidates, `design-brief.md`, `design-todos.md`, `architecture.md`, and `accuracy-audit.md`. It also checks the retained planning document and the current comparison and archive entry points. This is an independent judgment within the inherited model family. Agreement with another agent is not evidence from a different model family.

The scores assess proposed designs. They do not claim that either workflow is implemented or physically validated.

The score is a review aid, not a measurement of customer value. A would be preferable if the entire task were an exploratory diameter change against one imported pad. The current brief asks for independently traceable revisions and an auditable decision handoff.

## Keep from B

1. Keep one study document with two footprint sources, two selected pads, common operating conditions, and an explicitly sourced pad-face-gap requirement. Derive every lower diameter and source hash on the service.
2. Keep a focused study page beside the joint inspector. The existing force viewer can retain its old comparison meaning. A navigation link is sufficient integration for this phase.
3. Keep one service policy owner. The browser displays the accepted report and invalidates it on every relevant edit. It never recalculates the decision.
4. Keep native nominal solves for each declared geometry hypothesis. Two arms and two hypotheses require four native cases. That fits the existing seven-case archive bound without changing it.
5. Keep the gravity-off restriction for the two-pad approximation. The report must label its computational volume and mesh as an equivalent domain that includes fictitious volume inside metal. It must not present that mesh as the manufactured liquid solder surface.
6. Keep the static printable report and complete archive replay. A colleague should answer what changed, what conditional result follows, what reverses it, and what the lab must measure without running the browser.

## Graft from A

1. Use A's explicit eligibility order. Missing inputs, incompatible mask geometry, native failures, and scope exclusions precede any threshold interpretation. Preserve all failure records and explain the next measurement or supported operation.
2. Use A's explicit migration notice. A legacy case acquires neither a gap requirement nor boundary evidence. A legacy baseline can produce two arms with the same source, but the report must call that an upper-pad or volume comparison. It must not invent a second board revision.
3. Keep A's bounded text and payload limits. Two footprints justify a larger bounded study input. They do not justify removing the existing footprint limit or accepting arbitrary scenario matrices.
4. Keep the unloaded sphere plus published load correction as the visible analytical comparator. The loaded formula is the relevant cheap alternative for this aligned study.
5. Carry A's negative controls into B's acceptance set. Include pad-level and inherited mask margins, a positive pad override, missing board-mask evidence, nonzero offset, gravity, unsupported loading, stale asynchronous responses, and a failed native arm.

## Tighten before implementation

The analytical screen needs an explicit numerical policy. Start with A's stricter one-percent compression limit rather than B's provisional five-percent example. Record the cutoff as an implementation convention. It is not a published physical validity limit. Check formula and native nominal results at identical inputs. Report their difference and the declared acceptance tolerance. A nominal check does not validate the endpoint samples or every value between them.

Use exactly the declared volume points. Report three sampled volumes per arm and per hypothesis. Do not call their extrema a tolerance interval or infer monotonicity without a separate argument. A threshold reversal identifies a measurement need. Absence of a reversal means only that these sampled assumptions did not reverse the conditional result.

Keep the decision vocabulary small. Separate numerical failure, unsupported scope, missing evidence, assumption sensitivity, and a conditional nominal result. None is a manufacturing approval. If a formula sample falls near the criterion or disagrees with its native nominal comparator, return an unresolved interpretation instead of a confident preference.

Define common conditions consistently in the serialized shape and editor. B's sketch allows per-arm upper diameter and nominal volume while its initial usage describes common values. Per-arm values are useful for honest legacy migration. The editor can initially copy common values into both arms. The report must expose a difference if the imported study contains one. It must never describe differing inputs as controlled equal conditions.

Resolve unknown mask inheritance with an explicitly attributed evidence declaration. A standalone footprint cannot establish the final board artwork. Known smaller mask openings remain a rejection even when the user enters a declaration. A larger mask opening establishes geometric clearance only. Neither the parser nor the declaration proves wetting or physical pinning.

New study archives need model and solver source hashes as well as native identities. Replay should report numerical equivalence separately from exact provenance. It must regenerate requests and the decision from `study.json`, verify that each saved native case belongs to that study, and preserve the current rule against executing archived programs.

The coupon accuracy defects remain a prerequisite for broader credibility. Repair the immutable-input snapshot and numeric metadata handling. Make the declared input-uncertainty stop condition executable. The study must not present any coupon score as process qualification while those checks are missing.

## Concrete acceptance evidence

Use sources traceable to the actual 220 and 270 micrometer coupon geometry. Preserve their original texts and hashes. Check the downloaded native requests for 0.22 and 0.27 mm lower diameters. A pair of form labels or images is insufficient.

The retained `coupons/evidence/planning/plan.json` supplies an existing numerical reversal example. Literal-rim native gaps are approximately 223.319 and 207.522 micrometers. The two-pad gaps are approximately 178.203 and 167.314 micrometers. Both literal-rim results exceed 185 micrometers. Both two-pad results fall below it. The document explicitly marks these as planning assumptions and not physical data. The new workflow should retain that label and conclude that geometry interpretation must be resolved.

Demonstrate the browser edit and late-response checks on the actual running page. Download its actual archive. Extract and replay it in a fresh directory. Inspect both source hashes, the four nominal requests when both hypotheses are enabled, failed rows, sampled methods, and regenerated decision reasons. Tampering with a source must fail integrity verification.

Do not change the R1 physical experiment, allocation, or earlier negative physical evidence to make the new demonstration pass. A useful result can remain inconclusive. The product hypothesis is that this complete record improves the next engineering action beyond a spreadsheet. Practitioner demand and that advantage remain untested.

## Principles that changed the judgment

Model the Domain favors B's study containing two complete revision sources and one requirement. It avoids an override that separates a design's numerical diameter from its imported identity.

Laziness Protocol keeps the new operation on the existing local server, solver, and archive format. It rejects arbitrary scenarios, a material catalog, and a second general simulation system. It does not force a smaller diff when that diff fails the actual provenance requirement.

Prove It Works makes actual browser submission, native requests, exported sources, and fresh replay the completion evidence. A passing build or the existence of a report template cannot complete this workflow.
