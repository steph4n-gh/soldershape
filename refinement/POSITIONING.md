# Why a process engineer would use SolderShape

SolderShape helps a packaging or assembly process engineer review a board-pad revision before commissioning a physical test. Its useful deliverable is one reproducible comparison that states the design change, the conditional result, the assumptions that could reverse it, and the measurements needed next.

That value is a product hypothesis supported by a working local workflow. No customer has confirmed demand, saved an assembly iteration, or paid for it. There is no demonstrated moat yet.

## The narrow job

Use the tool when an actual BGA or CSP standoff question remains after package guidance and an assembly partner's review. Compare two real footprint revisions under identified solder volume, upper geometry and supported load. State the required pad-face gap and where that requirement came from. Use the report to choose an informative experiment or document why the present inputs cannot support a choice.

The browser's new design study keeps both imported footprints. It runs actual nominal equilibria, places the aligned analytical comparator beside them, and samples three declared solder volumes under named geometry hypotheses. The report withholds an unchanged-samples conclusion when a modeled assumption changes the threshold answer, a numerical case fails, or the analytical screen is outside its declared scope. It always labels physical accuracy unqualified.

The existing 220 versus 270 micrometer coupon is the concrete first example. Its literal-rim and finite-metal approximation produce opposite outcomes against a declared 185 micrometer pad-face criterion. The useful action is to measure finished metal and wetted boundaries in a controlled pilot. A nominal calculation alone cannot select a manufacturing-safe pad.

## When the simpler alternative wins

For a supported standard footprint, follow the package supplier and assembly partner. SolderShape does not override their qualification. For nominal aligned, lightly loaded joint height, a spreadsheet using the published formula is sufficient. Our retained nominal cases show no meaningful native accuracy advantage. For transient wetting, thermal reflow, warpage, fatigue or production yield, the current isolated equilibrium model does not answer the question.

Surface Evolver already supplies the underlying mathematics and broader examples. Interface Equilibrator advertises an established capillary CAD interface. Current availability does not create a durable competitive advantage. Primary source links and the exact comparison are in [alternatives.md](alternatives.md).

## What is useful now and what could become defensible

The current advantage is a specific engineering workflow. It joins footprint identity, explicit assumptions, numerical failures, analytical comparisons, an inspectable report and a prospective lab handoff. Each mechanism is copyable. A better interface or a language port would not change that fact.

The plausible future defense is a prospectively measured operating domain with a repeatable laboratory workflow. It would need characterized process inputs, independent held-out measurements, uncertainty budgets, documented failures and permission to retain the evidence. Its prediction errors would need to remain useful on new cases. One custom coupon cannot establish that domain. Confidentiality and process differences may prevent pooling or transferring the data.

No exclusive physics, proprietary validation dataset, laboratory relationship, customer adoption or switching cost is claimed. Product convenience is real work. It is not proof of durable defensibility.

## The next investment gate

Keep investment bounded until independent practitioners demonstrate an engineering action beyond the strongest simpler alternative. The proposed test recruits five packaging or assembly process engineers with a concrete current or recent decision. They first show their existing method and the applicable supplier guidance or analytical baseline. Then they inspect the SolderShape report.

Continue only if at least two supply a representable case and identify a useful change to their choice or test plan. Require at least one organization to assign an owner and obtain a real quote for an agreed validation test or paid case review. These are proposed business decision rules, not a representative market survey or current customer evidence.

Stop standalone expansion if no participant identifies an action beyond the simpler method. Also stop if all useful cases require physical effects outside this model. In that outcome, retain the verified tools as research and test-planning utilities.

No outreach, vendor contact, purchase or external publication occurred in this refinement.
