# Why someone would use SolderShape

This assessment checked primary sources on October 16, 2026. The capability baseline is local commit `f2cfd9b`. No customer, assembly laboratory, or software vendor was contacted. Competitor capabilities below come from their documentation. We did not run their commercial software.

SolderShape has a credible narrow use and no demonstrated moat. Its current useful result is an inspectable comparison and a test package that exposes what remains unknown. A claim of better physical predictions would exceed the evidence.

## The intended user has a specific exception to resolve

The strongest initial user hypothesis is an electronics packaging or assembly process engineer who owns a real BGA or CSP standoff problem. The engineer can obtain package-side dimensions, solder quantity, assembly load, and process information. The engineer must choose which board pad or solder-volume alternative to test against a stated pad-face gap requirement.

That is a narrower audience than every KiCad user. An ordinary board designer following a supported package footprint already has a strong default. AMD publishes package land dimensions and board design rules. It also directs customers to work with their contract manufacturer when they violate particular assembly recommendations. SolderShape cannot override those rules or a manufacturer's qualification. [AMD BGA design rules](https://docs.amd.com/r/en-US/am013-versal-pkg-pinout/Recommended-PCB-Design-Rules-for-BGA).

An inferred reason to care is that the engineer needs to justify a controlled exception and commission an informative test. The deliverable would state which decision the calculation informs, which assumptions control that decision, and which measurement could reverse it. No observed customer result establishes that this saves an iteration or earns a payment.

## What is already useful

The current work combines a KiCad input, an explicit isolated-joint model, a reproducible computation, and an outside-lab test package. Those artifacts help an engineer inspect the chain from a pad choice to a proposed measurement. [Workbench instructions](../README.md), [coupon package](../coupons/README.md).

The numerical evidence can also stop a misleading interpretation. The earlier high-offset physical force comparison failed. The independent aligned-height study finds a useful pad-size ordering but leaves physical residuals and unidentified process inputs. Neither result qualifies general manufacturing accuracy. [Physical verdict](../evidence/model-repair/report.md).

The actual coupon example makes that distinction concrete. Its literal-rim and finite-metal approximations put the same planning inputs on opposite sides of the 185 micrometer study threshold. That threshold concerns finished pad-face separation. It is not a validated underfill or whole-package clearance rule. The input values are artificial planning assumptions, not new measurements. [Coupon planning record](../coupons/README.md).

The most plausible future defense is a measured operating domain tied to a repeatable engineering workflow. Its content would include actual dimensions, liquid-state material data, cold and molten measurement distinctions, failed assemblies, and prospective prediction errors. Customer confidentiality may prevent pooling those data. Process variation may prevent transferring them. A growing dataset does not automatically make each new customer's prediction more accurate.

An exclusive solver, broad product-market fit, a proprietary dataset, customer switching costs, and a profitable service are all unproven. There is no defensible basis for a market-size or revenue claim.

## The smallest decisive local refinement

Make one decision brief for the real 220 versus 270 micrometer comparison. Give each design its own board-pad diameter or footprint identity. At the baseline commit, the browser comparison holds the selected board pad fixed and compares upper pad or volume changes. That restriction excludes the precise pad-change decision in the coupon.

The brief should include the requirement, the two candidates, the prescribed load or gap, input provenance, and the physical meaning of each length. Put the strongest analytical comparator beside the native result in its supported aligned low-load regime. Label sampled sensitivities as samples. Keep the literal-rim and finite-metal assumptions visible. Give no release verdict when input uncertainty or model mapping can reverse the threshold decision.

Acceptance means an engineer can open one artifact and answer four questions. What are we changing? What conditional result follows? What could reverse it? What must the lab measure next? A repeated native result alone is insufficient. The current planning example must produce a need for measurement instead of a claim that either pad is manufacturing-safe.

This is a proposal for the root refinement, not a claim that the new brief has been implemented by this research task.

## An external demand falsifier prepared for later use

Run a bounded study with five independently employed packaging or assembly process engineers. This is a proposed decision rule, not a statistically representative market survey. No recruitment or contact occurred in this task.

Each participant must bring a current or recently resolved pad or solder-volume decision, its actual constraints, and the method they would otherwise use. First show the supported supplier guidance and strongest applicable analytical baseline. Then show SolderShape's evidence brief. Record whether the brief changes a choice, changes the test they commission, or merely adds a picture.

Continue product investment only if at least two participants supply a concrete case the workflow can represent and identify a useful change to their engineering action. Require at least one participating organization to assign an owner and obtain a real quote for an agreed validation test or a paid case review. A favorable opinion or a willingness to try free software does not satisfy that gate. No payment amount is assumed.

Stop the standalone product expansion if none of the five can name an action beyond what the simpler baseline already supports. Also stop if all meaningful cases require package warpage, moving wetting boundaries, thermal history, or reliability predictions that the present model cannot support. In either outcome, retain the verified tools as research or test-planning utilities rather than adding unrelated features.

Experience First would focus the refinement on a reviewable engineering decision. Explain the Number keeps analytical agreement separate from physical qualification. Those principles change the proposed work from another solver feature to a bounded decision brief with an explicit stop condition.
