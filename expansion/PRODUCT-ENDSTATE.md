# The useful end-state

SolderShape should help a packaging or assembly process engineer turn a particular pad or solder-volume question into an independently reviewable experiment. The finished unit of value is a board-bound comparison, its missing input measurements, a prospective prediction, and a laboratory result that can reject it.

This phase extends that workflow in two directions. An attributed routed board supplies real package and pad identity. A matching hot-force experiment supplies a way to test the large-offset predictions. Neither direction establishes accurate physical forces, customer demand, package self-alignment, manufacturing yield, or a defensible data advantage.

## What an engineer can accomplish

An engineer can locate a real circular board pad, declare the unknown opposing rim, gap, liquid volume and material properties, and compare two isolated static equilibria. The exact native mesh and signed forces expose a possible tradeoff that an aligned height formula does not answer. Exporting source identity, assumptions and computation together avoids transcribing the problem into a separate laboratory brief.

The force package then asks a narrower question. Can an independently characterized, horizontally oriented molten bridge reproduce these fixed-rim forces within a stated uncertainty? The first purchase decision is a staged commissioning quote, rather than an instrument order. Failure of hot zero, full rim visibility, input uncertainty or stable wetting is useful evidence against attempting the confirmation test.

## The strongest practical alternatives

For a supported production footprint, package-supplier guidance and an assembly partner's qualified process remain the stronger default. For aligned, lightly loaded height, the published analytical approximation is sufficient in the regime already examined. For an expert capillary calculation, native Surface Evolver already provides the engine and broader freedom. General board inspection belongs in KiCad.

SolderShape earns a place only when its complete source-to-test handoff changes an engineer's choice or test plan. The board atlas is useful context. It is not an independent scientific advantage. A language port, a larger board, or a more elaborate renderer would not establish one.

## Evidence required before further expansion

1. A lab must accept the actual sensor attachment, optical access, thermal integration and achievable uncertainty. The prepared files alone do not satisfy this gate.
2. Commissioning must demonstrate the corrected hot force uncertainty and independent input uncertainty needed by the chosen endpoint. Instrument resolution is not that result.
3. Held-out physical observations must test frozen predictions without fitting surface tension or wetting assumptions to those same forces. Preserve failures, missing specimens and scope exclusions.
4. At least five relevant practitioners should compare a concrete case against their current method. Continue standalone product investment only if at least two identify a useful change to a decision or experiment, and at least one organization assigns an owner to a real quote or paid review. This is a proposed investment rule, not current demand evidence.

The plausible future defense is a reproducible, prospectively measured operating domain that remains useful on new cases. It would require permission to retain evidence, process-specific input quality, documented errors and independent replication. One fixture and one board cannot establish it. If the useful cases consistently need moving wetting boundaries, package warpage, transient chemistry or coupled load sharing, stop expansion of the isolated-joint product and retain the verified test-planning tools.

No lab outreach, purchase, fabrication authorization or public deployment is part of this phase.
