## Definition of done

A quote-ready matching force experiment with editable and inspected CAD, neutral exports, drawings, instrumentation/BOM, independently justified uncertainty gates, calibration/procedure, allocation, blank data templates and prospective analysis. A polished complex-board workflow links actual board geometry to real individual-joint calculations, editable assumptions and reproducible exports. Actual software and artifact checks pass. Physical validation, lab acceptance and fabrication release remain false until external evidence exists.

Scope is two local deliverables, not an unlimited new simulator. First test whether the required micronewton force contrasts are measurable at the required temperature and geometry. Compare two whole designs per major seam. Preserve the source, previous coupon, existing native recordings and failures at base65b9839. The user has no lab equipment, so provide an outside-lab package. No lab messages, orders, spending, deployment or fabrication authorization.

## Throughput checkpoint

Root owns project integration, design selection, quantitative controls, final claims and actual browser verification. A read-only architecture explainer grounds reuse. Independent fixture concepts address sensor and thermal boundaries. Independent board-demo concepts address useful decisions and geometry identity. Each delegate writes one disjoint report. Implementation owners receive separate directories or files after selection. Deterministic generators and verifiers handle repeated CAD, allocations, predictions and exports. All role settings inherit the parent model. Reviews do not establish cross-model agreement.

## Before-code acceptance

1. Derive force, gap, offset, volume and material uncertainty requirements from actual contrasts. Budget expanded uncertainty for the7.6uN upward sign and10.1uN lateral contrast. Show feasibility limits, not instrument resolution as accuracy.
2. The fixture physically represents the specified boundary conditions or declares the mismatch. Editable native CAD and STEP/DXF/PDF dimensions agree with machine-readable geometry checks. PCB outputs use actual KiCad DRC/export where required. No fabricated instrument specification or assumed lab acceptance.
3. Controls quantify compliance, drift, cross-talk, tare, wetting and equilibration. Independent specimens and run blocks prevent pseudoreplication. Hold-out predictions are frozen before force outcomes. Missing or poor measurements lead to inconclusive, never a pass.
4. Analysis consumes exact frozen bytes and records source identity. Synthetic self-checks are unmistakably labeled. No outcome-derived parameter fitting masquerades as independent validation.
5. A complex board has identifiable real package/pad coordinates and at least one useful comparison beyond aligned-height Eq9. No tiled-joint rendering claims a coupled solve. Unsupported geometry and model limits remain visible.
6. The running browser supports select actual board location, inspect native result, change a meaningful assumption, fresh computation and downloaded replayable study. Recorded versus live evidence is explicit. Exports preserve actual identity and failures.
7. Relevant existing checks, fresh independent review, extracted handoff verification and local commits complete the locally achievable task. Exact setup/replay commands and remaining physical/practitioner gates are delivered.
