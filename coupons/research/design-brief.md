# Design brief before candidate selection

Prepare an external-laboratory experiment comparing 220 and 270 micrometer board pads. The requester has no lab equipment. Produce editable boards, manufacturing exports, an upper coupon, a controlled solder specification, an assembly and metrology procedure, and prospective analysis.

Ground the design in evidence/model-repair/report.md and evidence/coupon-grounding.md. The nearest published comparison uses twenty joints, a 5 by 4 array, 0.5 mm pitch, 280 micrometer upper pads, and nominal 0.01745 cubic millimeter solder volume. These are reference conditions, not mandatory procurement targets.

Identify both finished metal heights and wetting reference planes. Do not silently transfer the paper-specific 34 micrometer correction. Resolve sphere size versus liquid volume, cap mass and per-joint load, pad sidewalls, thermal warpage, and cooled versus molten observations. Treat 185 micrometer pad-face clearance as a study question.

Compare two designs before CAD implementation. Judge identifiable inputs, practical fabrication and assembly, metrology access, confound control, and total complexity. Define independent cap units, paired and blocked runs, pilot gates, blinding limits, and conditions that stop confirmation. Custom parts and lab capabilities require written quotes. Do not contact suppliers or present availability as confirmed.
