What the physical comparisons established

Four published CSP standoffs were predicted from pad geometry, solder volume, and supported load without using measured heights as inputs. With the paper's pad-volume approximation and an assumed 0.48 N/m surface tension, absolute height discrepancies were 1.32 to 7.11 µm. The simple analytical calculation remains a strong alternative for aligned, lightly loaded joints. Unreported process uncertainties prevent manufacturing approval.

The earlier Josell force discrepancy remains unresolved. A forward upstream calculation predicts about 14 µm offset against a measured 31 µm under an inferred assembly load. Contact-line motion, package tilt, and load sharing remain outside this model. Use this tool to screen design assumptions and plan a physical coupon test.
