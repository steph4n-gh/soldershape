Declared design question

Pad-face gap is the separation of the wetted pad planes. It is not clearance from the board mask to the package body.

0.05 samples the nominal volume and volumes 5% below and above it. These are three points, not a tolerance bound or yield prediction.

This approximation adds equivalent pad cylinders to the computational domain, then subtracts both metal thicknesses from the solved height. Its mesh is not the physical solder shape. Gravity must be off.
