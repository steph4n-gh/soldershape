200 µm pads. Two liquid volumes.

FROM BOARD EXPLORER

Test the 400 µm board-joint question.

The ULX3S U1 / J16 example compares 40 and 56 nL at a 300 µm held gap and +240 µm offset. Its 330 µm trial changes the gap alone. The objective is lateral restoration without an upward separating force while a fixture holds the gap. It does not select the solder amount for a free package.

  1. Save the editable board case and fresh board-bound evidence. Keep the source-pad identity, exact inputs and numerical review with the test request.
  2. Measure the hot wettable rims, upper pad, liquid solder volume, held gap and offset. Identify the alloy, temperature, density and surface tension before freezing predictions.
  3. Adapt and qualify the 200 µm fixture for the 400 µm joint. Agree on force uncertainty, hot blanks, calibration and independent confirmation specimens before measuring hot force versus displacement.

The 200 µm kit is a starting design for the separate worked example. It is not a qualified fixture or ready fabrication package for this board question.

SEPARATE GAP EXPERIMENT

220 vs 270 µm pad comparison

These are two lower copper pad diameters on a twenty-joint height coupon. The design question is whether pad sizing preserves enough supported component clearance despite process variation. Its declared target is 185 µm. Wetting assumptions change the clearance outcome. Opposite arm variations can erase or reverse the predicted ranking. Neither size has production qualification, and this separate height test does not validate the board example's forces.
