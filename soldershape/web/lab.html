01 / MATCHED FORCE EXPERIMENT

200 µm pads. Two liquid volumes.

The fixture holds two circular wettable rims at a 150 µm hot gap. Independent bridges compare 5 and 7 nL at 40 and 120 µm lateral offsets. Separate specimens measure lateral and normal force. The primary question is the restoring-force contrast at 120 µm.

This matches the Worked example. It does not validate the 400 µm-pad assumptions in Board explorer. Your geometry or process needs its own measured-input plan.
