      WHAT STILL NEEDS MEASUREMENT

      Copper size is only one boundary.

      Actual wettable rim and mask opening, liquid solder volume, opposing package pad, hot gap and offset, alloy, temperature and surface tension. Board mask rules and assembly conditions are not qualified by this view.
