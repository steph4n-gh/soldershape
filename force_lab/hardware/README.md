# Q1 force fixture geometry

This is an outside-lab quotation package. Digital geometry is verified. Sensor acceptance, lab acceptance, fabrication release and physical validation remain open. Do not machine a vendor adapter or buy a sensor from this package alone.

The lab gets four neutral STEP assemblies, 16 individual STEP part files, editable CadQuery source, one dimension specification, dimensioned SVG sheets and two DXF boundary layouts. The two sensor configurations preserve horizontal specimen faces and gravity along negative Z. The vendor sensor head is deliberately absent. The two orthogonal adapter mounting datums establish a quotation envelope, not a qualified sensor mechanism.

## Reproduce

From the repository root, create a Python 3.13 environment and install the pinned CAD runtime.

```sh
python3.13 -m venv output/cad-env
output/cad-env/bin/python -m pip install -r force_lab/cad/requirements-lock.txt
output/cad-env/bin/python force_lab/tools/build_cad.py
output/cad-env/bin/python force_lab/tools/verify_cad.py
```

`force_lab/cad/spec.json` controls dimensions. `force_lab/cad/model.py` is the editable native CAD source. `requirements.txt` identifies the direct dependency. `requirements-lock.txt` records the tested transitive versions on macOS arm64 with Python 3.13. CadQuery 2.6.1 and OpenCascade 7.8.1.1.post1 generated the neutral files. The verification tool imports those actual STEP and DXF files. It checks solid validity, solid count, volume and bounds, and rejects solid interference. It also checks pad planes, source-relative offset, optical reserves, moving-load adjacency, moving mass and conservative tilt clearance. `manifest.json` binds exact generated bytes and the source. STEP/DXF creation metadata can change on a rebuild. The geometry checks use explicit tolerances rather than claiming byte-identical exports.

## Datums and assembly

The lower wettable metal face is Z=0. The upper face is Z=0.150 mm. Both islands have nominal diameter 0.200 mm. The assemblies use upper-center X=0.040 or 0.120 mm. There is no rendered solder solid in this mechanical assembly. The native solver owns liquid geometry.

The 0.0005 mm metal-film thickness is an illustrative envelope. It is not a qualified deposition stack. Its separate solid keeps the wet-face datums correct. Accordingly, the lower substrate top is Z=-0.0005, the upper substrate bottom is Z=0.1505, its top is Z=0.4505, and the stalk center is Z=1.0005 mm. Replace the thickness with a qualified measured process and rebuild before release. Do not change the wet-face gap to absorb the film.

The 2 by 2 by 0.3 mm transparent cap places its island 0.350 mm from the free positive-X edge and centered in Y. A horizontal 20 mm long, 1 mm diameter silica stalk ends 0.800 mm behind the island. A conformal bond joins the cap top to the stalk. A 2.5 by 1.5 by 0.3 mm cool tab and its end bond provide a supplier-approved sensor-tip attachment target. No secondary guide, force-bearing thermal lead, shield or clamp contacts this upper load path.

The 10 by 10 by 0.5 mm lower chip rests in a 10.2 mm square, 0.2 mm deep pocket in a 24 by 24 by 3 mm alumina thermal carrier. A 12 by 12 by 20 mm alumina pedestal isolates it from the cool base. Heater, retention, purge and chamber mating interfaces require the lab's released drawings. The 24 mm carrier is not claimed to fit the nominal 22 mm sample area of a stock Linkam stage. The design permits an accepted custom lab enclosure.

The base is 80 by 80 by 8 mm. Its four diameter 4.5 mm holes on a 60 mm square are internal fixture holes. Additional tap-drill geometry mates the custom bracket and shields. Drawings identify M4 and M3 tapping. Those holes are not an instrument-stage pattern. The L bracket has orthogonal mounting faces for the adapter blank. The shelf top is Z=26.700 mm, leaving clearance above the vertical blank. The vertical and horizontal blank positions do not prove an actual sensor tip reaches the tab. Supplier acceptance must establish that fact without changing the hot-joint datum.

The two polished stainless shield envelopes have diameter 4 mm noncontact apertures around the rod and separate base feet. Their inner bend detail and radiation performance remain quote items. They do not supply a structural bearing. The geometry leaves positive-X and positive-Y external optical cylinders 8 mm in diameter, starting 12.5 mm from the joint and extending 20 mm. Narrow gap-level rays reach the target. An 8 mm empty cylinder cannot pass through the solid specimen faces. These apertures reserve space for the lab's optical train. They do not establish microscope numerical aperture, depth resolution or complete contact-line visibility. Through-glass imaging must demonstrate the entire relevant rim before confirmation.

## Thin-film DXF meaning

`upper-cap-boundaries.dxf` and `lower-chip-boundaries.dxf` use millimeters. Each has one 0.200 mm circle on `METAL_ISLAND`, the same boundary on `NONWET_BOUNDARY`, the substrate outline on `SUBSTRATE_OUTLINE`, and a cross on `DATUM_ONLY`. The cross is a construction datum. It must not be fabricated as additional wettable metal.

The nonwet region is the substrate face outside the island and inside the substrate outline. The files define boundaries for a lab process engineer. They are not fabrication masks, Gerbers, a proven nonwetting treatment or an arbitrary tall copper pad. The lab must qualify metal composition, thickness, adhesion, dissolution, pattern fidelity, nonwetting surround and hot pinning. Actual circular rim dimensions and hot coordinates enter the prospective model inputs. A conventional PCB fab stack would add unsupported mask, roughness and wetting assumptions here. No PCB fabrication file is needed for these glass coupons.

## Mechanical planning results

The verified CAD has 15 closed solids per assembly and 16 neutral part files across both adapter orientations. The modeled moving parts weigh 40.394 mg using the declared planning densities. This leaves 59.606 mg to a 100 mg total moving-mass target, including any additional attachment. The target is not a sensor rating. The modeled dead weight is 396.132 microNewtons. A plus/minus 2 mN range does not establish cross-load or moment compatibility.

The modeled gravity moment about the cool-tab center is 4.390 microNewton meters. Adding a 124 microNewton transverse joint load in the worst sign gives 7.124 microNewton meters. These estimates exclude the actual sensor tip, extra adhesive, cables, thermal gradients and dynamics. Obtain supplier acceptance for the full force, moment, cross-load, temperature and overload envelope in both scalar configurations. Taring does not restore mechanical range.

The silica rod-only room-temperature estimate gives 1334.56 N/m transverse stiffness and 0.0929 micrometers deflection at 124 microNewtons. Uniform 200 K heating would expand it 1.88 micrometers. Those figures exclude sensor, bond and tab compliance. They require local hot-rim coordinate feedback and assembled calibration.

The silica estimates use density 2.201 g/cm3, Young's modulus 72.5 GPa and mean expansion coefficient 0.47e-6/K from [Heraeus fused-silica material properties](https://www.heraeus-covantics.com/brands/clear-fused-quartz). The remaining material densities are explicitly declared engineering planning values. The bond and metallization densities describe unqualified envelopes, not an accepted material stack.

At the initial 0.1 degree tilt envelope, the conservative minimum upper-cap-to-lower-plane clearance is 0.147120 mm. The conservative rod-to-shield radial clearance is 1.460730 mm. These prevent collision. They do not establish geometric uncertainty or parallelism adequate for the force endpoint. Sensitivity and actual hot metrology set the final tolerances.

## Release blockers

Obtain accepted sensor envelope and tip attachment drawings. Confirm both orientations leave the joint horizontal, cover the full coarse/fine motion range and have no bypass force path. Obtain sensor calibrated stiffness, force and moment response, cross-load limits, overload limits and temperature limits. Approve bond chemistry and the complete moving mass. Approve the heater, chamber, atmosphere and optical train. Qualify the wettable island and surround, full-rim visibility and actual hot geometry. Pass transfer calibration, hot blanks, drift and uncertainty gates before physical confirmation.

These remain real engineering uncertainties. A valid STEP assembly cannot resolve them. If the lab rejects the carrier moment or the boundary cannot be observed, preserve that failure and quote the lateral-only large-signal alternative or a separately justified sensing architecture.
